Helping Young Children Regulate

An evidence-informed ELS staff learning resource. Source check: 6 October 2026.

Scope and review

An evidence-informed learning resource prepared for the Inspired ELS context. It is not an Inspired-approved policy, clinical qualification, safeguarding course or physical-intervention training. Follow your setting’s current safeguarding, inclusion, first-aid and emergency procedures and local legal requirements. A completion record shows course participation only.

Research supports responsive relationships, thoughtful environments and supported skills practice. Evidence for specific early-years self-regulation programmes is limited and uneven; co-regulation-specific intervention research is still developing. These practical examples are original teaching illustrations, not a validated treatment protocol or a promise of particular outcomes.

French and Kreol Morisien are draft translations awaiting fluent early-years and safeguarding specialist review. Local school procedures and individual plans remain authoritative. The course does not claim endorsement by any cited organisation.

Six modules and reflections: approximately 48 minutes. Assessment: 10 minutes. Acknowledgements: 2 minutes. Optional videos and team practice add time. Written reflections are not graded. The named certificate requires 16/20 and all six safety-critical answers correct, plus the remaining online requirements.

Course record and privacy

Use fictional examples only. Adult participant details and responses are stored in the current browser, with no central register and no automatic email service. The public repository contains course content only. Staff download their own records and choose whether to share them with the confirmed Head. Self-entered identities and device-local records are not independently verified.

The SVG/CSS illustrations and all fictional scenarios are original teaching materials. Videos open on official provider pages; provider availability and caption options may change. The on-screen summaries and full explainer transcripts provide text alternatives. Downloaded certificate and practice-guide PDFs are image-backed, not tagged accessible PDFs; the course and results text provide readable alternatives.
