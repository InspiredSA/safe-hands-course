# Helping Young Children Regulate – Level 1

Inspired ELS staff development · Content/assessment version 1.1 · 6 October 2026

A practical, evidence-informed course for adult teachers, teaching assistants and caregivers. Core classroom cases concern children aged 2–6. Clearly labelled infant guidance is an extension for staff whose setting and authorised role include infant care; it is not handling, feeding or safe-sleep training.

## What changed in version 1.1

The original edition contained useful safety principles but insufficient age differentiation, teaching detail and applied judgement. This revision uses a direct review of ZERO TO THREE, Child Mind Institute, Conscious Discipline, NCPMI, Harvard and Raising Children Network to change the taught decisions, rather than merely add citations.

- Six rewritten modules with about 4,900 words of lessons and activities, retaining familiar fictional classroom cases
- Broad infant/toddler/preschool planning guides and developmentally different adult responses
- Explicit before/during/recovery/later procedures, short scripts and demonstration prompts
- A taught visual-routine sequence, accessible help/break practice and a full teaching-to-routine example
- Original age-adapted games with easier versions, participation options and stop cues
- Worked factual observation and routine support-plan examples, including urgent-help overrides
- Ten applied assessment replacements comparing plausible responses, while retaining twenty questions and six critical safety items
- Original classroom toolkit: age cards, decision aids, now/next and help/break/stop examples, a supervised-space checklist, a worked plan and blank discussion prompts
- Five optional official video pages with purposeful adult viewing tasks and text alternatives
- Forty-one source entries, module-level source mapping and explicit evidence/access/copyright limits

The schools’ no-hand-dryer constraint is retained. Leo’s sensory example is a noisy classroom play-to-tidy-up transition.

## Scope and evidence

This is not an Inspired-approved policy, accreditation, clinical training, a validated treatment protocol or physical-intervention training. No cited organisation endorses this course. Follow current school procedures, individual plans, applicable local requirements and role/training limits. The source organisations provide a mixture of practice guidance, activity resources, programme materials and evidence synthesis; those categories are distinguished in the course notes.

Conscious Discipline is a named programme. Selected resources inform optional exploration, not acceptance of every brain-state explanation or a claim that a particular picture, script or breathing practice has a proven neural mechanism. Source suggestions involving forced touch/gaze, withholding care, ignoring distress, fixed calm-space exit requirements, improvised restrictive techniques or clinical treatment are not adopted. Provider materials should be previewed and selected through the course safeguards, not followed indiscriminately.

The age groups are flexible planning guides, not milestone tests. The 3–6 guide includes six-year-olds. Harmless movement, AAC, gestures, home-language cues and individual sensory preferences are respected. Ordinary access adjustments need not wait for a diagnosis. Serious danger, suspected medical emergency and safeguarding concerns override a routine co-regulation plan.

Planned core time is about 75 minutes: six ten-minute modules, a twelve-minute assessment and three-minute acknowledgement. Optional videos, extended toolkit exploration and later team practice add time. This estimate is not a completed staff timing study.

## Privacy and public access

The repository and GitHub Pages site are public. No participant, real child or school incident records are committed. The site has no backend registration endpoint, analytics or automatic email service. Hosting providers may log ordinary traffic. Browser-local data and self-entered identity are not tamper-proof or centrally verified.

Use fictional examples in course responses. Current and historical details, responses, quiz history and certificate records remain in the browser. Downloaded reports, JSON and prepared emails include those adult-entered details. Staff review and send an email manually; downloading an `.eml` does not send it. If an email client cannot edit the file, attach results/certificate to a fresh email. Never place confidential child, health, family or incident details in the course. Use approved school records instead.

On a shared device, use a private window, download needed records and close it. Clearing all site data on the shared Pages origin can remove progress for every course; download all needed records first. The update does not remove the old storage key.

## Evidence notes

### Professional guidance and original classroom applications

ZERO TO THREE, Child Mind Institute, NCPMI, Harvard’s activity resources and Raising Children Network offer professional explanations, synthesis and practical examples. They inform the taught adult decisions, but do not constitute trials of these scripts, games or tools. All course scenarios, wording and tool layouts are original; no provider endorses the course.

### Branded materials are examples, not independent proof

Conscious Discipline is a named educational programme. Selected resources inform reflection on predictability, adult-supported spaces and feelings vocabulary. Its branded steps, characters and brain-state explanations are not adopted as a diagnostic framework or copied into the course. Child Mind’s Pre-K materials are also identified as a programme, not represented as an independent evaluation of this training. Free access does not mean unrestricted reuse.

### Independent research and limits of inference

EEF’s evidence synthesis and research agenda address the wider evidence base separately from provider practice materials. The early-years intervention evidence remains limited and uneven; co-regulation-specific and younger-child evidence has gaps. A Conscious Discipline-linked report describes an adjusted observational association in 45 classrooms, not a randomised test of individual practices. None of these sources independently validates this 75-minute course, guarantees an outcome or establishes a neural effect from a script.

### Local safety and individual judgement remain essential

Research-informed support does not replace approved infant care, individual communication plans, supervision, health assessment, emergency response or safeguarding. Source age bands differ; this course’s broad planning groups are not milestone tests. The course selectively excludes forced touch or gaze, withholding care, ignoring distress, improvised restrictive techniques and clinical treatment instructions.

### Access, copyright and optional viewing

New provider pages and documents were checked on 6 October 2026. The new video additions were checked at page, companion-resource and transcript level; their playback was not independently tested. Each course video has an adult viewing task and text alternative. Conscious Discipline’s schedule download may require a free account. Provider graphics, stories, templates and logos are linked rather than reproduced or translated.
