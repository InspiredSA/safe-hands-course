# Helping Young Children Regulate – Level 1

Inspired ELS staff development · Content/assessment version 1.1 · Concrete examples and printable communication cards, 7 October 2026

For adult teachers, teaching assistants and caregivers supporting children from 12 months through the early years. The practical guides cover 12 months to under 18 months, 18 months to under 3 years, and 3–6 years, including six-year-olds. These are flexible groups, not milestone tests. Follow each child’s agreed plan and the duties/training authorised by the school.

## Course contents and time

- Six lessons, each with a worked school example, a quick choice and a short written practice task
- Twenty final quiz questions; at least 16 correct and all six key safety questions correct are needed
- Critical questions: 6, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 18
- Three original illustrated explanations with play/pause, step controls, readable captions and full text
- Five optional official video pages with adult viewing prompts and text alternatives
- Classroom tools: age guides, what to do before/during/after distress, now/next examples, help/break/stop signals, a quieter-space guide, a filled Leo plan and blank planning prompts
- Downloadable A4 communication-card PDFs in English, French and Kreol Morisien, including adult directions and blank templates
- A separate team practice guide generated in English, French or Kreol Morisien
- Named certificate PDF, full results text and an email file that the participant reviews and sends manually

Planned core duration remains about 75 minutes: six ten-minute parts, twelve-minute quiz and three-minute acknowledgement. Optional videos, toolkit exploration and further staff practice add time. This estimate needs a staff pilot; it is not an observed course-completion time.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

The course is evidence-informed staff learning. It is not an Inspired-approved policy, accreditation, clinical qualification, validated treatment or permission to use physical intervention. No source organisation endorses this course. Teachers/caregivers should follow current local procedures, individual plans and their training limits, and ask for the appropriate trained help.

Keep necessary care and adult support available. Do not force touch, eye contact, speech, breathing, isolation or an apology to obtain help or rejoin activities. Do not teach or improvise holds. Emergency and child-safety reporting steps take priority over a routine support plan. The course teaches no feeding, handling or sleep technique and does not diagnose children.

The six requested source families are ZERO TO THREE, Child Mind Institute, Conscious Discipline, NCPMI, Harvard and Raising Children Network. Sources include professional guidance, activity collections, named programme materials, research synthesis and observational evaluation. Those categories and limits remain distinct. Original course scripts, drawings and plans are not provider-validated materials. Source suggestions are selected carefully; not every suggestion on a linked page is adopted. Provider artwork, stories and templates are linked rather than reproduced or translated.

## Evidence notes

### Professional guidance and original classroom applications

ZERO TO THREE, Child Mind Institute, NCPMI, Harvard’s activity resources and Raising Children Network offer professional explanations, synthesis and practical examples. They inform the taught adult decisions, but do not constitute trials of these scripts, games or tools. All course scenarios, wording and tool layouts are original; no provider endorses the course.

### Branded materials are examples, not independent proof

Conscious Discipline is a named educational programme. Selected resources inform reflection on predictability, adult-supported spaces and feelings vocabulary. Its branded steps, characters and brain-state explanations are not adopted as a diagnostic framework or copied into the course. Child Mind’s Pre-K materials are also identified as a programme, not represented as an independent evaluation of this training. Free access does not mean unrestricted reuse.

### Independent research and limits of inference

EEF’s evidence synthesis and research agenda address the wider evidence base separately from provider practice materials. The early-years intervention evidence remains limited and uneven; co-regulation-specific and younger-child evidence has gaps. A Conscious Discipline-linked report describes an adjusted observational association in 45 classrooms, not a randomised test of individual practices. None of these sources independently validates this 75-minute course, guarantees an outcome or establishes a neural effect from a script.

### Local safety and individual judgement remain essential

Research-informed support for children from 12 months does not replace approved care procedures, individual communication plans, supervision, health assessment, emergency response or safeguarding. Some reference sources cover younger babies; these remain background rather than under-12-month practical assignments. Course age bands are broad planning guides, not milestone tests. The course excludes forced touch or gaze, withholding care, ignoring distress, improvised restrictive techniques and clinical treatment instructions.

### Access, copyright and optional viewing

New provider pages and documents were checked on 6 October 2026. The new video additions were checked at page, companion-resource and transcript level; their playback was not independently tested. Each course video has an adult viewing task and text alternative. Conscious Discipline’s schedule download may require a free account. Provider graphics, stories, templates and logos are linked rather than reproduced or translated.
